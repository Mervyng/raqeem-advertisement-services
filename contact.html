Frequently Asked Questions

How rapidly can Raqeem deploy a live international campaign?

For performance digital advertising and brand acceleration, our rapid deployment sprint goes live in 10 to 14 business days. Full brand identity overhauls and 3D anamorphic billboard productions typically span 4 to 8 weeks.

Do you execute bilateral Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs)?

Yes. Strict client confidentiality is mandatory at Raqeem. We sign mutual NDAs before any sensitive brand assets, commercial numbers, or proprietary strategies are exchanged.

What geographic markets does Raqeem specialize in?

While our creative hub is centered in Dubai UAE, our media buying and digital advertising architectures manage active client campaigns across Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, the UK, Europe, and North America.
